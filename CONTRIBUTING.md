This is in progress; not sure how I want contributions yet. For now, here are the following guidelines:

- Thoughtful bugfixes or minor improvements are appreciated, but no new features.
  - Pyxie Tarot is lightweight by design, and I am not looking to introduce bloat.
- AI-generated code is permitted on the condition the submitter understands and can defend or explain the code generated.
  - No "vibe-coded" submissions from non-engineers will be accepted; please respect this.
- I am willing to comment on PRs to provide direction on needed fixes, but I will not be taking over others' submissions.
  - If the submitter is unable or unwilling to improve their submissions on my request, it will instead be rejected.

Thank you for considering giving to this project! I truly appreciate you 💛
